Computer Networks pre-submission checklist

[ ] Use the elsarticle manuscript and editable .tex source files.
[ ] Upload main manuscript and highlights as separate files.
[ ] Verify title, affiliations, corresponding-author email, and author order.
[ ] Confirm the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
[ ] Confirm all figures are cited, readable, and supplied separately if requested.
[ ] Check abstract, keywords, declarations, funding, conflicts, and data/code statement.
[ ] Include CRediT author contributions and generative-AI disclosure when applicable.
[ ] Deposit data/code in a repository (Option C), or explain why sharing is impossible.
[ ] Remove all FCS-specific class files, logos, headers, and submission identifiers.
[ ] Check that every citation has a bibliography entry and vice versa.
[ ] Run pdflatex/bibtex twice and resolve undefined references and overfull boxes.
